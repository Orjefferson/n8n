Privacy Policy

App: product-manager-content-assistent

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Who we are

This application is a personal automation project that drafts LinkedIn posts from public RSS feeds, sends drafts for approval via Telegram, and may publish approved content to the operator’s own LinkedIn profile.

2. Data we process

  • Public RSS article metadata (title, link, excerpt).
  • Draft post text generated by an AI provider.
  • Telegram chat identifiers configured by the operator.
  • LinkedIn OAuth tokens and profile identifiers needed to publish on behalf of the operator.

3. How data is used

Data is used solely to generate, review, and publish content for the operator. We do not sell personal data. We do not use LinkedIn or Telegram data for advertising.

4. Storage

Drafts and credentials are stored on the operator’s local infrastructure (for example Docker volumes and environment variables). Access tokens may be retained until revoked or expired.

5. Third parties

  • Google Gemini (or configured LLM) — content generation
  • Telegram Bot API — approval messages
  • LinkedIn API — OAuth and publishing

6. Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact the app operator through their LinkedIn profile associated with the developer application.
